This game is inspired by Sub Rosa, No Big Deal, Goofy Gorillas, and a little bit of Human Fall Flat. The 'core ideas' are listed below.
- Physics based characters
	- Includes climbing and simple but physics based combat, probably just punching and grabbing.
	- Make sure that the map is suited to the climbing so it doesn't feel out of place-- have interactable things around the map for the player to use in order to seem super cool.
	- Also ensure players can grab onto moving things like cars.
- Physics based weaponry
	- Includes melee weapons and firearms, both of which are physics based.
	- Maybe include throwing weapons too?
- Inventory
	- Probably a 10 slot inventory system like Minecraft.
- Actually good [[Low Level Content]], as opposed to Sub Rosa having pretty bad content but being fun due to the mechanics and the fact that there is content in the first place.
	- Preferably include a variety of puzzles, multi-step missions, and less 'random' feeling-- you shouldn't have the following happen, which does in sub rosa:
		- Get a lab 
		- Find the disk on the floor
		- Put it in the computer
		- Find out that it's at the lab you're currently at
		- Find it on the first computer, high value project
		- Bring it back to your base which is 1 block away
		- Make $250k due to it being high value
		- THEN,
		- Get a town car
		- Take an hour to find it and barely get it in time (while getting ran over by NPC cars-- our cars should not be as annoying as sub rosa's)
		- Bring it back to base
		- It's a lab across the map
		- Find it on the final computer you search
		- It's a trade mission and you have 200k corp but nobody else is on so you only make 5k when you would have made 200k if it calculated based on your corp.
	- Also have a variety of actual things to do in something like world mode-- you should be able to do MORE missions than just:
		- Town car or Lab
		- decrypt disk
		- go to second lab
		- get project
		- go home
	- Also have a variety of modes, similar to how Sub Rosa has CTF, Boxing, World, Round. DEFINITELY have a prop hunt. Also have a Racing mode, which has both car and foot races.
- The theme is essentially the following:
	- In a sort of hyper-1970s mafia world, where tech has advanced but culture has not, you play as robots created by -insert our equivalent of megacorp here- in order to make more money. Every player works for this corporation, but you are all working to make the most money for the corporation, hence the name 'Internal Affairs'.
	- Visually, it will be a sort of brutalist mixed with cities. The robots will be similar in aesthetic to the MRVNs from Titanfall, but clothed in suits as if they were business men.
