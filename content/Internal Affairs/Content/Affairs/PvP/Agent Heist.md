There are two variants of this mission, both which will be active at once. This is also the highest paying mission.

# Defense
- You must defend a building against an incoming wave of players.
	- Specifically, you must take your Intel (which is in a briefcase on the ground), hide it however you wish, and defend it from the opposition.
		- The Intel is not allowed to leave the building until 15 seconds after the mission has begun.
	- This is also an 'opt-in' mission. The teams must be balanced.
		- If the teams are not balanced, the team with less players will receive 2 NPCs for every player they are missing.
- If you kill all of the opposing players, or the time runs out, you win.
# Offense
- You must break into the building and steal the Intel from the Defending team.
- If you manage to take the Intel outside of the building and bring it to YOUR building, you win.
- You are given a burner phone (no ID) that has the 'Tracker' app and no other apps. 


# Notes
- Sometimes, the Intel may be swapped out for some other valuable thing (such as a [[Paper]] or a rare Weapon). Everything will still work the same way.
- This mission has a timeframe that it can be done during. The reward for each team will be proportional to the time-- if it is an exceptionally short timer, the offense will make LOTS of money if they win, while the defense won't make much.
