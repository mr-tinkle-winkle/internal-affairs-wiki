Marty "Martyr" Robot, or Marty R, was a robot that essentially ruined robot society.

Marty was a robot that committed a crime, resulting in robots losing their rights and being reduced to machines.

Marty will be a large figure in billboards-- both in offense to (e.g. "ROBOTS ARE NOT PEOPLE, LOOK AT MARTY R.") and in defense (e.g. "IS ONE BAD DAY WORTH THE LOSS OF A PEOPLES RIGHTS?")
