Combat is pretty simple. Using the [[Independent Hand Movement (Pointing, Throwing, Grabbing, Interacting, and Parkour)]] system, the player can already 'slap' other players-- however, if the player presses C (for Combat), they will enter Combat Mode, which makes LMB a 'Punch' (uses the hand that corresponds with the direction you are looking-- left hand is used if turning right, and vice versa), and RMB a 'Block'. Pointing in combat mode will also punch with the specified hand instead of pointing, incase the player wants to punch with a specific hand.

The player can, in fact, still grab in combat mode-- but can only activate grabs while pointing. The priority for pointing will be reversed during combat mode-- as in, while grabbing something, moving your hand will move the object itself rather than your free hand.

The player can also kick on X. This will kick the same leg as punching would (turning right = left kick, turning left = right kick). If the player holds X, it will hold out their leg.
