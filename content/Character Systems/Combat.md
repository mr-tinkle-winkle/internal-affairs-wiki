Combat is pretty simple. Using the [[Independent Hand Movement (Pointing, Throwing, Grabbing, Interacting, and Parkour)]] system, the player can already 'slap' other players-- however, if the player presses C (for Combat), they will enter Combat Mode, which makes LMB and RMB punches, and both at the same time a block.

The player can, in fact, still grab in combat mode-- but can only activate grabs while pointing. The priority for pointing will be reversed during combat mode-- as in, while grabbing something, moving your hand will move the object itself rather than your free hand.

The player can also kick on X and C. This will kick the same leg as punching would (X for left, C for right) If the player holds the kick button, it will hold out their leg. Kicking does not have a 'double action' as punching has blocking, but you CAN drop-kick.

The Boxing Idle will hold the hands at about chin height and relatively centered. Punching will have the hand move directly to the camera in a straight line. Kicking will have the hand move as close to the camera as possible in a straight line, after a short 'Chamber' period. This allows stomping. Kicking will also activate a slight Gyro effect on the player if they are currently planted on the ground, in order to allow cooler kicks.
