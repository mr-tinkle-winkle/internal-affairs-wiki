Refer to [[Expressions]] for the Voice Volume system.

- This game has proximity voice chat.
- The player will have the pitch of their voice changed by their view pitch (how high/low they are looking relative to themselves).
- The players' voice is dampened by walls.
- The player's text font volume (light/regular/bold) is based on their Voice Volume.
- The player's text font is based on their team.
	- When using a phone, the font used on the receiving end is the font of the phone owners' team.
