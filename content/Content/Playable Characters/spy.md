# Functions
- pressing F makes the player camoflauge
	- this makes the spy invisible to both players and NPCs. their movements are still audible. they exit invisibility if they take damage.
- pressing G brings up a drone.
	- players can grab this drone.
	- the spy controls this drone with a toggle on G.
		- double tapping G will cause the drone to beep for a short period of time, and then explode.
	- if the spy is not in the drone, the spy has the drone view in the bottom left corner of their screen.
- pressing H brings up the spy disguise menu from tf2. the player can disguise as any character on any team, and can also disguise as an npc. if disguised as on the same team as an npc (be it an actual team or a civilian team), the npc will not be alerted upon seeing the player.
	- taking damage will cause you to undisguise, while dealing damage will not.
