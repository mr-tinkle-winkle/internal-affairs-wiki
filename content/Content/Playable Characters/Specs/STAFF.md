This character is exclusive to [[Games/Internal Affairs/Content/Companies/Pluribus]] members, specifically those with the 'Staff' ranking.
# Functions
- pressing F will cause an explosion where your right hand is pointing
	- this explosion does not damage you, but will damage other players
- pressing G will toggle flight.
- pressing H will enter "tank mode"
	- disables falling over for your character-- it will specifically just activate an extremely strong gyro
	- increases the players' strength and mass, making them deceptively strong
		- this essentially makes them stronger than the size changer's heavy mode, but as large as the size changer's normal mode.
	- allows you to 'super jump' with CTRL + Jump, which launches you in the direction of your camera
	- gives the body extremely strong regen and super high HP
	- turns an LED on, which disrupts the players' "pure" color scheme by being red
# Design
- Has a 'pure white' color scheme
