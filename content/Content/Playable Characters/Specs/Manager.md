This character is exclusive to [[Games/Internal Affairs/Content/Companies/Pluribus|Pluribus]] members, specifically those with the "Owner" rank.

# Functions
- Has constant ESP on all players, although it is typically very transparent, only becoming very opaque when entering Aim Mode.
	- This ESP also applies to the map-- you see a dot on the map that corresponds to each player, with their nametag above their dot. Clicking on their dot on the map will teleport you to them.
	- Looking at any object will give you a 'dev view' of it (which will appear in one of the free corners of the screen).
		- This will show you any applicable stats for it; health, strength, character, mass, inventory, everything.
- Immune to ALL social credit changes-- any money gained automatically assumes the maximum social credit.
- All damage taken will be reflected back at the perpetrator.
	- The player will still receive the knockback.
- Holding CTRL will, ontop of the default 'righting' posture given, SIGNIFICANTLY increase the mass and strength of the player.
	- This will make the player essentially immune to knockback.
