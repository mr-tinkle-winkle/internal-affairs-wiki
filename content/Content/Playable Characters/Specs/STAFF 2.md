This character is exclusive to [[Games/Internal Affairs/Content/Companies/Pluribus]] members, specifically those with the "Staff" ranking.
# Functions
- Pressing F will 'silence' a player.
	- This prevents the player from moving or using their abilities, and significantly lowers their voice volume.
	- Attempting to silence a player that is currently silenced will reduce their voice volume to 0.
		- Silencing them again in this state will revert the voice volume change.
			- You can repeat this as many times as you wish.
- Pressing G will 'grab' the player you are looking at with your mind. This will exactly attach them to your cursor.
	- The Keybinds for this will match the keybinds for the [[Gravity Gun]].
- Pressing H will turn the player invisible and give them flight, allowing them to both go invisible and teleport around.
	- Invisible Voice Volume and Voice Volume are separate.
		- To be clear, when you enter invisibility, your Voice Volume will be Normal by default, regardless of your actual normal Voice Volume.
		- The two voice volumes will be kept separate; changes to volume while invisible will disappear when becoming visible, and will reappear when becoming invisible again.
	- Visible players can not hear you if your voice volume is the default.
	- If using Yell voice volume, visible players can hear you at a distance equivalent to 10x the usual Yell distance.
	- If using Whisper voice volume, the player closest to you can hear you, and only that player.
		- This is independent of distance-- in theory, you could speak to a player anywhere on the map with this.
	- Other invisible players can hear anyone that is currently invisible, regardless of distance.
