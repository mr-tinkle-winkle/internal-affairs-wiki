# Functions
- F will shoot a fireball
	- this will not damage the wizard.
- G will shoot a 'transmutation' beam
	- this will turn whatever it hits into a random item for 15 seconds.
	- this works on ANYTHING with the sole exception of the map.
- H will stop time in a radius around the wizard. the wizard will be able to move during stopped time.
- player can cast spells with their voice by saying "Fireball", "Transmute/Transmutation", "Halt/Time Stop/Pause"
