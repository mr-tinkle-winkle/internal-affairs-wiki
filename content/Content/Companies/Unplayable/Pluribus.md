This is the 'megacorp' of Internal Affairs. Developers, Staff, and Testers can choose to play as a member of Pluribus in any server, and being a member of Pluribus will give you access to any of the Pluribus Characters. It will also make you unable to lose Social Credit by any means-- anything that would lower your Social Credit will raise it by the amount that it would have lowered.

Pluribus does not have a consistent color scheme, although it trends towards 'pure' colors like white and black.

Pluribus is the oldest company, dating back to the 'old days'.

Pluribus as a word comes from the phrase 'e pluribus unum', which means "out of many, one."
