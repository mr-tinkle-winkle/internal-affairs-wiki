During a Storm, there will be thunder and tornados.
- Having a metallic object out (be it a gun on your back or a metal item in your hand) will have a persistently reactivating chance of it beginning to conduct electricity. 
	- If the item begins to conduct electricity, there will be a fizzling noise that will progressively increase in volume. This will take 5 seconds.
	- While conducting electricity, the gun will have an electric effect.
	- After the gun finishes conducting electricity, it will be struck by lightning. This will do significant damage to (if not killing) the player holding the item. It will also have an AoE explosion around the item.
- There will be a constant chance of tornados.
	- If a tornado forms, it will begin to form at a random spot on the map. 
	- When the tornado forms, it will take about 25 seconds to fully form, and there will be an extremely loud swirling noise accompanying it.
		- Tornados will have a random size, which will dictate how long they take to form and how large (and powerful) they will be.
	- The tornado will act as you would expect with the physics objects.
