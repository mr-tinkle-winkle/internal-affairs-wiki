The Ring has a few things players can do.

# Sanctioned 1v1s
- Players (both the fighters and the spectators) can bet on fights.
- This is exclusively fights between players; both players enter the ring via tubes, and battle to the death, and are let out via tubes in the floor.
# AI Xv1s
- The player fights X number of AIs at once.
- The player can choose the value of X, with the default being 1.
