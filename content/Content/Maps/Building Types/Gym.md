The Gym has multiple areas. They also may be separate buildings depending on the map.
# Soccer Court
- Has two goals and a unlimited soccer ball supply (via a ball cart))
# Net Court
- Allows for both volleyball and tennis playing.
- Volleyballs, tennis balls, and rackets are off to the side.
	- The balls are unlimited in the same manner as the Soccer Court.
# Ring
The Ring has a few things players can do. The Ring is a hexagonal boxing ring, with a button that lowers a cage to turn it into a cage fight. 
# Sanctioned 1v1s
- Players (both the fighters and the spectators) can bet on fights.
- This is exclusively fights between players; both players enter the ring via tubes, and battle to the death, and are let out via tubes in the floor.
# AI Xv1s
- The player fights X number of AIs at once.
- The player can choose the value of X, with the default being 1.
# General Notes
- Any 'ball' items will despawn if more than 10 of them are in the world at once.
- Any 'racket' item will despawn if the player that created it spawns two rackets.
