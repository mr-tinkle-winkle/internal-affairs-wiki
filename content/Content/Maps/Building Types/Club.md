These buildings are similar to Jackbox. The players can create a 'Party', which will be a group of players. The players are ranked on the side of the screen in most to least points. They have the following group games:

# a
- Every player is given a prompt.
- The player creates a funny answer for that prompt.
- In 1v1s, prompts are voted on and gain points based on the ratio of voters.
# b
- Every player is put into duos (optional, can do singles).
- Each duo is linked up with another duo.
- Linked duos create speeches and simple slideshows for each other.
- The players are scored based on the total time cheered in the crowd between all individuals.
# c
- Players (duos or singles) are given a scenario to act out by another player (duo or single).
- Actors receive points based upon how fast the crowd guessed their presentation.
- Guessers receive points based upon how fast they specifically guessed their presentation.
- Guessing can be done in text or via voice recognition. 
    - If a correct guess is made, confetti poppers will explode and party horns will play. These will be fast, and a giant red arrow will point to whoever got it right.
    - Text guesses will be invisible if correct.
    - Voice guesses will have the 'CORRECT!' indicator seen by other players not appear for 4 to 30 seconds.
