Every Civilian NPC has stats. These all range from 0 to 10.
- Aggression
	- This determines how long an NPC will follow you after you have angered them. This will also determine how easilly an NPC will get angry with you.
- Health
	- Determines how much HP the NPC's limbs have.
- Strength
	- Determines how strong the NPC's attacks are.
- Hated Companies
	- Determines the company the NPC hates. If the 'perpetrator' of their anger is somebody from that company, the NPC will have their Aggression stat effectively multiplied against that company.
		- This means that they will become more angry with those that work for companies they hate, and that they will become angry with them easier.
