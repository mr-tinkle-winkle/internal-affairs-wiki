Relatively self explanatory, it's a helicopter.
- Can hold up to 6 players (2 in the 'pilot' area and 4 in the middle)
- Has a tether that can be spooled out downwards and reeled back in, both manually.
	- The tether (specifically the end) can be picked up by players, and can also be attached to ANYTHING by using the tether on it.
		- The player can tether themselves by clicking on themselves.
