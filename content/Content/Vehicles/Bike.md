A bike has multiple unique features.

- If in the back seat and someone is in the front seat, CTRL + W will make you stand up and aim over them.
- Both players will automatically lean out in the direction they are not moving their camera in (look left = lean out right, vice versa— changeable in settings)
- Instead of a handbrake, the bike has a 'Piston Jump'. This is a piston on the bottom of the bike. The player visually activates it by kicking a second set of pedals down, and activates it by jumping.
