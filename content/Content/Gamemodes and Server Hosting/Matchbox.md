This is based on the ['Matchbox' Minecraft ARG.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=II5h5V1Cov0) The [rules](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dmwAJjVQRNc) are as follows : 

- There is one (customizable number) 'Spark'.
	- The Spark can mark one person by grabbing them.
	- The Spark can swap places with a player by pressing J. This will open a menu where the Spark can choose WHO to swap with.
	- Both Marking and Swapping are completely silent.
- There is one (customizable number) 'Medic'.
	- The Medic can mark one person by grabbing them.
	- A player marked by the Medic CAN NOT die to the spark.
		- If they are saved by the medic, they will be notified in the meeting that they were saved. Nobody else will be told.
- Every player plays as the same Character.
- The game is played in 10 minute rounds.
	- At the end of the round, the player marked by the spark will die, and a meeting will commence.
		- Meetings are 5 minutes long.
			- The exception to this is the first minute, which is 1 and a half minutes.
			- Players have the option to 'skip' a meeting, which will skip straight to the voting.
		- At the end of the meeting, players have the option to vote out a player of their choice, or to not.
- The game, by default, takes place in a randomly generated assortment of premade rooms.
	- All Rooms are also available as custom maps under 'Matchbox Rooms'.
		- The host is also able to use a 'custom' Matchbox Map by inputting a seed manually.
	- The host is able to use a seed to choose the map layout if they wish.
	- The rooms will have things to do, so the players do not get bored during the 10 minute rounds.
- Every player gets one 'Identifier'.
	- The Identifier is a [[Magnum]] with one bullet. If a player shoots another robot with this, ontop of knocking said robot over with the force of the bullet, it will show their nametag above their head.


The Host can change the following settings.
- Random Names : T / F
- Character Played As : any character
- Random Character : T / F
- Number of Sparks : 1+ slider
- Number of Medics : 0+ slider
- Round Length
- Percentage Required to Execute a Player
- Percentage Required to Skip
