There are the following variations of Money:
# REAL
- 1 dollar
	- Gray
- 10 dollars
	- Bluish-Green in color
- 100 dollars
	- Green in color
- 1000 dollars
	- Golden in color
# FAKE
Each money has a fake variant. It can have any of the following flaws (minimum of one).
- Incorrect Art
	- Make absolutely sure that it is obviously incorrect if you focus on it-- not just 'different' than the normal money, but obviously weird.
- Incorrect Value
	- These would be SPECIFICALLY weird values, like a 2 dollar bill, a 17 dollar bill, a 101 dollar bill, a 1984 dollar bill, etc-- each has their own possible dollar values.
- Wrong Phrase
	- IF we give money a phrase on it, the phrase can be wrong.
