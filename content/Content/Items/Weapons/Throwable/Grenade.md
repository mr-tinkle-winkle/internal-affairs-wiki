The grenade is a very versatile tool, allowing the player to toggle all of its' features.

Grenades are loaded and unloaded like a gun, with the 'ammo' being the pin.
The grenade will blow up 5 seconds after leaving the players' inventory without the pin.

A player can enter the menu to customize their grenade by spitting on it. Each of these changes has a visual change.
- Sticky
	- On / Off
- Bouncy
	- On / Off
- Damage
	- On / Off
