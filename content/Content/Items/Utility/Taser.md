This is sort of a melee, but due to it's uses being half utility and half weapon, it resides in the Utility category.

- The Taser will 'short-circuit' any robot that it comes into contact with. 
- This will cause the robot to take no damage, but become knocked out for a minimum of 15 seconds.
- If the Taser is used on a robot that is already short circuited (or otherwise not conscious), it will 'revive' them.
	- This does not work on dead robots, only incapacitated robots.
