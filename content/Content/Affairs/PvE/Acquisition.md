The player must find and acquire some important item, and bring it back to a designated drop-off point.

The player will be doing this against exclusively NPCs.

Acquisition is a two-step mission, with the first step being dedicated to finding the intel, and the second step being dedicated to acquiring the intel.

The variations of the mission are as follows:
# Intel Locating
### Interrogation
- The player must find NPCs that work for the company provided, and interrogate them. 
	- Interrogation involves dealing damage to the enemy and waiting. Every time the enemy receives damage, there is a chance they will 'crack' and give up the location. If it is a In Transit Acquisition, they will give you a burner phone.
### Hacking
- The player must reach a computer. The building that the computer is in will be guarded.
- The player must crack a document that has been shifted with a Caesar Cipher.
- If it is a Heist mission, the document will reveal the location of the building.
- This can not be an In Transit mission.

# Intel Acquisition
### In Transit
- The player is given a burner phone with the 'Tracker' app.
- The tracked location is a car with the intel, and the player must get the intel from the car before the car deposits it at a drop-off point.
### Heist
- The player must enter the building, find the intel, take it, and leave.
- There may or may not be guards.
