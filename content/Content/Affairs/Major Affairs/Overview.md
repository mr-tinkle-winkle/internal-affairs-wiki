Major Affairs are 7 day long missions with 4 parts.
# Introduction
- A problem is presented to the player. The player must find the source of the problem.
- This will be things like 'Our Company lost 5 billion dollars yesterday. Find out who did it.'
# Preparation
- The player learns of a location, a robot, an employee, whatever.
- This, in the context of the above Introduction, would be something like 'The employee was being blackmailed by a larger corporation. We found the location of the blackmailers.'
# Fixing
- The player ACTUALLY fixes the problem at hand.
- This, in the context of the above Preparation, would be something like 'Go into the place, shoot up the blackmailers.'
# Cleaning Up
- The player cleans up and ensures the problem is fixed.
- This, in the context of the above Fixing, would be eliminating stray blackmailers, getting the money back to the company, and taking out the head of the blackmailing group.
