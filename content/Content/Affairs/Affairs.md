Affairs is a system that supports 'Minor Affairs', 'Daily Affairs', and 'Major Affairs'.
# Minor Affairs
- Minor affairs are essentially what the player does whenever they have nothing else to do, or need a little money on the side. 
- There is an unlimited amount of these, although there is a limited amount that can be accessed at once in the server. 
- When one is completed, another opens up.
- These missons are single part.
# Daily Affairs
- Daily Affairs are the small things that the company will request you to do in a single day.
- This is the 'normal' missions, akin to the missions given in Sub Rosa.
- Everything unspecified under the Affairs folder is a Daily Affair.
# Major Affairs
- Major Affairs are large scale, high profit missions.
- There is one Major Affair per week per company.
- These missions are NOT visible to the other companies, only to the company you are on.

# All Affairs
All affairs reset at the end of the week.
