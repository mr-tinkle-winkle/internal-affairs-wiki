# Slot Declaration
- The player has two hand slots and ten inventory slots. 
- The two hand slots are visible above the other slots, and the ten inventory slots are relatively out of the way.
- The left hand slot corresponds to the left hand, and the right hand doing the same for the right hand.
# Function
- By pressing the specific number of an item (1-10), the player will take from their inventory and put the specified item into their right hand.
	- Holding Shift during this will cause it to go into the left hand.
	- If the players primary hand is occupied, the item will go to the left hand.
- By pressing the specific number of an empty slot, the player will put the item in their hands into the inventory.
	- This will prefer the left slot, although holding Shift will make it use the right slot.
	- The right slot will also be used if the left slot is empty.
- By pressing ALT + Click, the player can drop the corresponding item (LMB for left, RMB for right).
